📊 Health Score

Measures access to nearby urban services using proximity-based spatial analysis.

🩺 Health
  • 💊 Pharmacies (≤1 km): 15 pts
  • 🩺 Primary Care (≤1.5 km): 25 pts
    Medical offices, centers & dispensaries
  • 🧪 Laboratories (≤2 km): 10 pts
  • 🩻 Imaging Centers (≤2 km): 10 pts
  • 🏨 Clinics (≤3 km): 15 pts
  • 🏥 Hospitals (≤5 km): 25 pts

👥 Population Adjustment

To better reflect real health conditions, the final score is adjusted using population pressure.

Each clicked location is linked to its corresponding Delegation, from which an official population value is retrieved. This value is normalized to produce a Population Pressure Score between 0 (low pressure) and 1 (high pressure).

Final score formula:

Final Score = Accessibility Score × (0.7 + 0.3 × Population Pressure)

• 70% of the score reflects access to health services (POI proximity)
• 30% reflects population-induced urban pressure

Total Score Meaning:

  • ≥70 → Good access 🟢
  • 40-69 → Moderate access 🟠
  • <40 → Poor access 🔴
